Mr. Dipak Kumar Gupta

FOUNDER & CEO

( DK SIR )

"Cinema is not just entertainment, but a powerful art form to transform emotions and thoughts. Our vision is to bring global recognition to real talent and take Indian Cinema to new heights with advanced technology."

Mr. Dipak Kumar Gupta (DK Sir) is a visionary entrepreneur, film producer, and technology leader. He is the Founder & CEO of DK ATOVITT. Under his leadership, the company has not only initiated the production of high-end 4K and VFX-based movies but has also laid the foundation for a revolutionary digital and Web3 ecosystem.

He believes that extraordinary talent is hidden in every corner of India, which only requires a transparent and right platform. With this vision, he launched 'DK Film Production' and 'DK Connect', providing direct opportunities for new actors, creators, and tech enthusiasts to grow and succeed.

His upcoming ambitious horror-romance movie, 'Yakshini', is fully prepared to set new benchmarks in Indian Cinema. Under the visionary guidance of DK Sir, DK Atovitt is building a mega ecosystem uniting art, technology, and business, which will set a global example in the near future.
