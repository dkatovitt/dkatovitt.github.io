PUBLIC NOTICE & DISCLAIMER

IMPORTANT WARNING: Beware of Fake Casting Agents and Fraudulent Offers.

It has come to our attention that certain unscrupulous individuals and agencies are falsely claiming to represent DK Atovitt Production House. These individuals may approach aspiring actors, singers, writers, and technicians, offering false promises of casting in our upcoming movies, web series, or music videos in exchange for money.

1. No Fees for Auditions

DK Atovitt strictly does NOT charge any fees for auditions, casting, or talent selection. We have not authorized any casting agency, coordinator, or individual to demand money, registration fees, or portfolio charges on our behalf. Any such demand is a scam and a fraudulent act.

2. Official Communication Channels

All official announcements regarding casting, auditions, or job openings will only be made through our official website (dkatovitt.github.io) and our verified official social media accounts. Any email communication from us will exclusively come from addresses ending in @dkatovitt.com.

Unauthorized use, reproduction, distribution, or piracy of our content is a punishable offense under the Copyright and Trademark laws of India. Strict legal action will be taken against violators.

4. Liability

DK Atovitt, its Producers, Directors, and employees shall not be held liable or responsible for any loss, damage, or financial fraud suffered by any individual who engages with fake agents or unauthorized persons claiming to represent us.

Report Fraud
