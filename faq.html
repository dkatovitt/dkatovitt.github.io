Help & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is DK Atovitt Production House?
DK Atovitt is an official movie production house dedicated to creating Bollywood horror films, romantic dramas, soulful music videos, and unique entertainment content.
How can I apply for upcoming movie auditions?
You can apply for auditions by clicking on the "Auditions" section on our homepage or by visiting our Login/Signup page. We never charge any money for auditions.
Are there any fees for casting or registration?
No! DK Atovitt strictly does not charge any fees for auditions, casting, or talent selection. Beware of fake agents asking for money. Please check our Public Notice page for details.
Where can I watch DK Atovitt movies and song videos?
All our official trailers, full movies, and music videos are hosted on our official platform and linked directly through our website. You can watch them anytime in the Movies and Songs sections.
How can I partner or collaborate with DK Atovitt?
If you are interested in media partnership, sponsorships, or business collaborations, you can reach out to our team directly via email at contact@dkatovitt.com.
How do I contact customer support?
You can write to us anytime at contact@dkatovitt.com for any queries, support, or feedback.
