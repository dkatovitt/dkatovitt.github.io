Help & Frequently Asked Questions

What is DK Atovitt Production House?
DK Atovitt is an official movie production house dedicated to creating Bollywood horror films, romantic dramas, soulful music videos, and unique entertainment content. Our current flagship project is the horror-romance film "यक्षिणी (Yakshini)".
How can I apply for upcoming movie auditions?
You can apply for auditions by visiting our Auditions page or by navigating to the "Audition" tab on our Login/Signup page. We welcome actors, singers, and crew members.
Are there any fees for casting or registration?
No! DK Atovitt strictly does not charge any fees for auditions, casting, or talent selection. Beware of fake agents asking for money. Please check our Public Notice page for more details.
Where can I watch DK Atovitt movies and song videos?
All our official trailers, full movies, and music videos are hosted on our platform. You can find them under the Trailers and Movie Songs sections via our main menu.
How can I partner or collaborate with DK Atovitt?
If you are interested in media partnerships, sponsorships, or the ATVT/Kalpamrit business ecosystem, you can reach out to our team directly via our Contact Us page or email us at dipakatovitt@gmail.com.
How do I contact customer support?
You can write to us anytime at dipakatovitt@gmail.com for any queries, support, or feedback, or chat with us instantly using the WhatsApp button on the bottom right of your screen.
