Privacy Policy

Welcome to DK Atovitt Production House. This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you visit or interact with our website (dkatovitt.github.io).

1. Information We Collect

When you visit the Site, we may collect certain information about your device and your interaction with the Site. If you sign up for auditions, newsletters, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and any other details you provide in forms.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you navigate the website, your IP address, browser type, and time spent on pages (collected anonymously for analytics).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various purposes, including:

  • To communicate with you regarding upcoming movies, casting calls, and auditions.
  • To improve our website's functionality and user experience.
  • To respond to your inquiries or support requests.
  • To maintain the security and safety of our website.

3. Sharing Your Information

DK Atovitt Production House respects your privacy. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may only share information if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of DK Atovitt, our users, or others.

4. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites (such as YouTube for our trailers and songs, or social media platforms). Please be aware that we are not responsible for the privacy practices of such other sites. We encourage our users to read the privacy statements of every website that collects personal information.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide against loss, theft, and unauthorized access. However, no internet transmission is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time in order to reflect changes to our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The updated version will be posted on this page.
