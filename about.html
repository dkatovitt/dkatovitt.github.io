The Visionary

Mr. Dipak Kumar Gupta

Founder & CEO ( DK SIR )

With a profound passion for storytelling, cinema, and soulful music, Mr. Dipak Kumar Gupta (affectionately known as DK Sir) established DK ATOVITT with a singular, powerful vision: to bring fresh, engaging, and high-quality entertainment to the global audience.

His dynamic leadership and deep understanding of content creation drive the company's ambitious projects. From the spine-chilling horror-romance of 'Yakshini' to chart-busting musical tracks, DK Sir ensures that every project touches the heart of the audience. He strongly believes in empowering new talent, fostering raw creativity, and building a transparent, scam-free ecosystem where true art and artists can thrive.

About DK ATOVITT

A New Era of Entertainment

DK ATOVITT Production House is a dynamic and forward-thinking cinematic company dedicated to creating impactful, high-end content. We specialize in producing diverse genres—from gripping horror and romantic dramas to emotional music videos and thrilling web series.

We are not just a production house; we are an entire ecosystem. By partnering with leading networks like Kalpamrit, ATVT, and Earn with DK Sir, we are constantly pushing the boundaries of traditional filmmaking and digital content distribution.
